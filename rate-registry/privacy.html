Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

WhatIff (“we”, “us”) runs an anonymous, information-only home-loan rate registry. Borrowers share the rate they actually got; everyone else sees anonymised aggregates. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We aim to collect as little as possible.

1. Who we are

WhatIff is an independent service. We are not a bank, lender, broker, direct selling agent (DSA) or loan service provider (LSP), and we are not paid by any lender. We do not arrange, facilitate or apply for loans.

2. What we collect

  • The rate you submit — your bank, interest rate, the year and size of the loan, its original tenure, an optional outstanding balance, rate type, how you got the loan, employment type and credit-score band. This is stored to build anonymous aggregates. No name is attached to it.
  • Your sign-in — to add a rate you sign in with Google or with an email and password, through our authentication provider (Supabase Auth). We receive an account identifier and your email address (and, for Google, basic profile info). This is used only to keep out spam, duplicate and fake submissions. It is never shown to other users and never attached to the figures we display.
  • Email for follow-ups — if you choose to receive a negotiation template or a savings calculation, you give us an email address. We use it to send you that material and, later, an optional one-time follow-up asking how it went. You can ignore it or ask us to delete it.
  • Technical data — a random identifier stored in your browser to prevent accidental duplicate submissions, and cookieless, aggregate visitor analytics (via Umami) that do not identify you.

We do not collect your loan account number, PAN, Aadhaar, address, phone number, or any document. Please don’t enter such details anywhere on the site.

3. How we use it

  • To show anonymised aggregates (medians, ranges and counts) so borrowers can compare.
  • To keep the data honest — detecting outliers and preventing spam, duplicate or fake submissions.
  • To send you material you explicitly asked for, and an optional follow-up.
  • To understand overall usage in aggregate.

4. What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share your identity, email or individual submission with lenders, brokers, agents or advertisers.
  • We never display an individual rate or contact detail — only aggregates.

5. Where it’s stored

Data is stored with our infrastructure providers — Supabase (database and authentication) and Vercel (hosting) — who process it on our behalf. Access to raw data is restricted; the public website can only read aggregates, never raw rows.

6. Retention

Anonymous rate data is kept to power the aggregates. Account and email details are kept while they’re needed for anti-spam and follow-ups, and removed on request.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you (your account and any email you gave us). Resubmitting on the site also supersedes your earlier entry. To make a request, contact us below.

8. Children

The site is intended for adults (18+) and not directed at children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date will change. Continued use means you accept the current version.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: contact@whatiff.in.

This policy is provided for transparency and is not legal advice. See also our Terms of Use.
